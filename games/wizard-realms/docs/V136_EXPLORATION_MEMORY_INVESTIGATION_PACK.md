The browser, watchdog and owned PIDs were closed; 5194 was untouched. The fresh journal priority was visibly correct.

Renderer RSS repeatedly rose with actions and movement, then fell with natural idle: settled 338128 to idle 306672 KiB; oak arrival 609776 to idle 454544; south movement 691616 to idle 521424; bridge approach 726992 to idle 551136; bridge build 610624 to idle 448240; deck move 646880 to idle 373664; post-claim idle 339952. Used JS heap rose into roughly 95 to 146 MiB during action phases and returned to roughly 60 to 70 MiB; separate GPU RSS stayed about 129000 to 153000 KiB. The strongest classification is transient JavaScript/native allocation, not retained-JS or GPU growth. There is still no source-level byte owner to justify a production patch. V135's 878672 KiB guard stop is plausibly cumulative temporary pressure from longer continuous inputs, not an established permanent leak.

This paced 15-minute route is a useful scoped success, not a long-session or continuous-run memory pass. Keep the 870400 KiB early stop for future tests. The next performance experiment, if prioritized, should compare matched continuous and chunked movement on an already discovered route while recording pending-frame count, save phase and allocation stacks, then change only the measured owner. Do not reduce scenery, relax checked validation, or raise the guard merely to pass.
