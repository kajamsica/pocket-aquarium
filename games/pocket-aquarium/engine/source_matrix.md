Sources that appeared upstream only as ancillary examples and do not support a selected claim were not promoted into this downstream control set.

## Authority Classes

| Code | Authority class | Use rule |
|---|---|---|
| `STD` | Measurement, unit, or technical standard | Controls definitions and conversions within its stated system. |
| `GOV` | Government or intergovernmental technical source | Preferred for public definitions, water treatment, environment, and institutional procedures. |
| `VET` | Veterinary or animal-care authority | Preferred for welfare, disease-boundary, quarantine, and broad water-quality screening. |
| `PEER` | Peer-reviewed primary study or review | Supports mechanisms, observations, and scoped experiments. |
| `EXT` | University or aquaculture extension | Supports engineering and husbandry mechanisms, with system-scope limits. |
| `WEL` | Government or established welfare guidance | Supports ethical gates and species-care dimensions, not universal numeric biology. |
| `FAC` | Public aquarium, laboratory, or restoration protocol | Supports a named facility practice or operating profile only. |
| `TRADE` | Professional ornamental-fish care guidance | Supports a husbandry convention or species-group example, not a universal minimum. |
| `MFR` | Manufacturer manual or support document | Supports only that product's documented mechanism, limit, or failure behavior. |
| `COMM` | Commercial educational material | May support optional player hints only when a stronger source does not establish a universal range. |

## Validation Rules for Downstream Authors

1. Cite the normal Markdown URL from the source registry beside a factual or numeric statement, or cite the exact claim ID and preserve the source mapping during assembly.
2. A source ID supports only the listed claim and stated scope. It does not license nearby unsupported values.
3. Any new numeric value, species minimum, product claim, medical instruction, or legal restriction requires a new accepted source and a source-matrix revision.
4. Facility, trade, manufacturer, and commercial values must retain their authority labels in prose and tables.
5. If sources disagree, preserve the conflict or select by explicit applicability and authority. Never average silently.
6. After any artifact revision, V1 must sample factual and numeric claims against this exact evidence revision and rerun namespace, welfare, equation, and cross-artifact consistency checks.
