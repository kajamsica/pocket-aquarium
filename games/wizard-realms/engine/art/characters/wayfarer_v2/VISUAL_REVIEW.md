# Wayfarer v2 male candidate: NO-GO

The approved character sheets are visual targets, not source textures. The
original male Blender candidate in this directory is a structural pipeline
probe. It is **not accepted art** and must not replace the playable avatar.

## Evidence

- Concept: `../../concepts/wayfarer-male-concept-v4.png`
- Turnaround: `male_front.png`, `male_three_quarter.png`, `male_side.png`,
  `male_back.png` (650 x 820, Eevee, 24 samples)
- Editable source: `wayfarer_v2_male.blend`, generated by
  `author_wayfarer_v2.py -- male`
- Export: `wayfarer_v2_male.glb`, `male_glb_report.json`, `male_manifest.json`
- Single Blender process: 2.78 seconds, 398 MB maximum resident set size,
  1.93 GB macOS peak memory footprint, no competing renderer
- Geometry: 17,618 triangles, 124 mesh pieces, 24 exported materials
- Rig: 17 deform joints, seven exported equipment sockets with `role` extras,
  and Idle, Walk, Turn animation channels present in the GLB

## Visual assessment against the approved sheet

The candidate preserves the broad violet and gold palette, long robe, belt,
diagonal strap, and satchel. Its silhouette and surface quality remain well
below the approved target:

- The face is assembled from separate cheek, nose, eye, and ear primitives.
  Profile and expression lack the coherent sculpted adult anatomy in the sheet.
- The hair is a collection of obvious tapered tubes; the rear scalp is mostly
  exposed, while the concept has continuous layered hair masses.
- Sleeves are oversized smooth cylinders. The torso, shoulder cowl, and robe
  skirt read as simple intersecting shells rather than tailored, draped cloth.
- Boots, hands, and satchel lack the shaped seams, folds, fingers, and leather
  surface variation that make the concept convincing.
- There is no authored UV or hand-painted texture, sculpt/bake detail, or
  cloth deformation. Flat materials cannot carry the approved detail level.

This is a **NO-GO** for visual promotion. A female version from the same
procedural method would repeat the failure, so none was produced. Reaching the
approved look needs a character sculpt and retopology pass, authored hair and
cloth forms, UVs and painted/baked surface maps, then rig and animation polish.
Those are original art tasks; no third-party asset or paid service was used.

## Asset workbench and next pass

Open `?workbench=wayfarer` in the running Wizard Realms app. This isolated
playground loads the current GLB beside the approved sheet and rejected
turnaround. Inspect front, side, back, top, and three-quarter views; play,
pause, slow, or scrub Idle, Walk, and Turn; then check wireframe and skeleton.
It does not read saves or change the playable avatar.

The next candidate should begin with a bounded face, hair, and sleeve sculpt
study before building another full character. Use coherent sculpted volumes,
retopology, UVs, and painted or baked detail rather than additional primitive
pieces. Once that study visually matches the approved sheet, complete the robe
and equipment silhouettes, transfer the established 17-bone and seven-socket
contract, and review Idle, Walk, and Turn in this same workbench. Only a
visually approved male asset should become the basis for a female model or an
in-game character replacement. A valid GLB or rig alone is not an art pass.
