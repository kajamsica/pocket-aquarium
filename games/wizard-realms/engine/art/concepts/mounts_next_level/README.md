# Wizard Realms: proposed mount concepts

This is a 2D reference gallery for future art-direction decisions: 15 ground mounts and 10 flying mounts. These are proposals, not playable creatures, approved 3D designs, or assets integrated into the game. No pull request was opened for this gallery.

Start with [ground_contact_sheet.png](ground_contact_sheet.png) and [flying_contact_sheet.png](flying_contact_sheet.png) to compare silhouettes and travel roles. Open any image in [renders/](renders/) at full size to inspect its empty saddle, handholds, and foot supports. [catalog.json](catalog.json) records each mount's name, body plan, travel niche, proposed acquisition hook, palette, and generation prompt. The [generation receipt](generation_receipt.json) records the exact image hashes and validation settings.

The concepts draw on the approved Wizard Realms illustrated style and broad mount-riding genres, but are intended as original designs. A still render cannot prove riggability, safe rider clearance, animation, care mechanics, or in-game scale. The reviewer accepted all 25 for human comparison, while flagging four later 3D questions:

- Silkspan Spider: resolve rider restraint and clearance between the moving legs and underslung sling.
- Lanternbelly Octopod: establish the creature's forward orientation before rigging and camera design.
- Lanternkeel Heron: check stirrup clearance against the breast, legs, and wing stroke in a mounted pose.
- Prismclast Rover: clarify the living anatomy so it does not become a stone vehicle in 3D.

Selection for 3D production and any gameplay integration require separate approval and validation.
